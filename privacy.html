Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026

This policy explains what information DeDe & Two Penguins ("we") handles in its Roblox games, on this website and in Kick and Run Uploader, our internal tool for the YouTube channel @kickandrunroblox.

1. Kick and Run Uploader and YouTube API Services

Kick and Run Uploader is a private command-line tool used only by us, the owners of the YouTube channel @kickandrunroblox. It uses YouTube API Services to upload short gameplay videos of our own games to our own channel and to read the statistics of that channel's videos (views, likes and comment counts). It is not offered to other people and authorizes only our own channel.

By using it we are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies to the data that Google processes. See also our Terms of Service.

  • What it accesses: our channel's basic information, the videos we upload and their public statistics.
  • How it is used: only to publish our own videos and to see how they perform.
  • Storage: the authorization token is stored only on our own computer. Statistics are read on demand and are not stored on any server.
  • Sharing: YouTube data is not sold, shared with third parties or combined with other data.
  • Revoking access: access can be removed at any time from the Google security settings page.
  • Retention and deletion: the tool keeps no copy of YouTube data, so there is nothing to delete besides the authorization token. When access is revoked or the tool is no longer used, the token is deleted from our computer within 7 days.

2. Our Roblox games

Our games run on Roblox, and Roblox's own Privacy and Cookie Policy applies to your Roblox account. Inside our games we keep only:

  • Game progress (for example coins, levels, items and football stars), linked to your Roblox user ID and stored with Roblox's game data services.
  • Friend invites: if you joined through a friend's invite, the Roblox user ID of that friend, so the invite reward is given only once.
  • Gameplay statistics sent to Roblox's built-in analytics (for example levels reached and items bought), which we see only as totals and use to improve the game.

Your Roblox display name and score can appear to other players in the game, for example on your team's sign and on leaderboards. All purchases are handled by Roblox; we never see payment details. We do not ask for your real name, email address, age or location, and we do not sell or share game data.

3. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no accounts, forms, cookies, analytics or advertising. GitHub may keep standard server logs as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

4. Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Our games only keep the game data described above, linked to a Roblox user ID.

5. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. Questions or requests about your data can be sent through the contact page.
